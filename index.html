Sports isn't just entertainment for me, but a constant puzzle of strategy, speed, and skill. I'm Daivik Darbar, and I write under the pen name Gaffers Notebook, exploring tactics, governance, business, and culture across the sporting landscape — from the pitch to the pit lane.

I aim to bring clarity to complex topics often overlooked in mainstream coverage. Whether it's dissecting team strategies, analysing competitive regulations, or highlighting overlooked narratives, I bring data, context, and curiosity to stories that deserve more than a headline.

Outside writing, I've spent time in sports operations, content strategy, market research, and analytics. I'm now based in Melbourne, studying a Master of Business (Sport Management) at Deakin and working towards a career in the Australian sports industry, where sport, data, and storytelling meet. I also played competitive football as a goalkeeper, representing Kickstart FC U-16 in Nepal against Red Star FC Jhapa and the Nepal U-16 national team in 2017 — which gives me a ground-level appreciation of the game I write about.
